FAQ

Answers before
you join.

Easy Equb is an app that digitizes the traditional equb. You join a group, contribute each cycle and a draw picks the winner. Everything is simple, secure and transparent.

An equb is a traditional savings group. Members put in a fixed amount each cycle, and one member receives the pot each round. Easy Equb brings that tradition to your phone.

Verify your identity in your profile. Then open Browse, pick an equb and tap Join. You can start contributing right away.

You can choose a Standard equb or a Personal · 100 Days equb. Contribution cycles are daily, weekly, bi-weekly or monthly. Use the filters in Browse to narrow the list.

By draw. A draw picks the winner each cycle, so no one has to decide by hand.

Verification helps everyone in an equb know who they are saving with. Once your identity is verified, you can join and contribute to equbs.

Yes. Easy Equb is free to download from Google Play. Check an equb's details before you join it.

Open Profile, then tap Help Center to contact our team. You can also change the app language from the same screen.
