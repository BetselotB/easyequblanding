# Details

The small things that make this site feel finished. People notice them without naming them; apply the ones that fit your project.

- **Frosted glass**: 1 surface blurs what's behind it (backdrop-filter), usually headers, menus and overlays.
- **Considered underlines**: Links set their own underline offset rather than the browser default.
- **Shaped edges and fades**: 67 clip-path shapes and 20 masks cut or fade edges instead of hard rectangles.
- **3D CSS**: 156 elements use perspective or preserve-3d for depth without WebGL.
- **Selection colour**: Selected text is rgb(255, 255, 255) on rgb(186, 186, 185) (::selection).
- **Focus ring**: Keyboard focus shows as 2px solid var(--color-gray-800), offset 2px (:focus-visible).
- **Styled scrollbars**: Scrollbars are restyled to match the interface.
